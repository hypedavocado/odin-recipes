Msmen

Msemen is a traditional Moroccan flatbread that is crispy on the outside and soft on the inside. Perfect for breakfast or as a snack, enjoyed with honey, butter, or a cup of mint tea.

Ingredients (8 pieces)

  • 300g all-purpose flour
  • 100g semolina
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 7g dry yeast
  • 200ml lukewarm water
  • 100g butter (melted)
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, semolina, salt, sugar and yeast in a bowl.
  2. Gradually add the lukewarm water and knead into a smooth dough.
  3. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Divide the dough into 8 equal balls.
  5. Flatten each ball and brush with melted butter.
  6. Fold the edges inward to form a square shape.
  7. Flatten again with your hands or a rolling pin.
  8. Fry in a pan on medium heat with a little oil until golden brown on both sides.
  9. Serve warm with honey and butter.
