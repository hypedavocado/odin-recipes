Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian comfort food made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, and creamy béchamel. This hearty dish is perfect for a cozy family dinner.

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 250g lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 400ml tomato sauce
  • 300ml béchamel sauce
  • 150g grated mozzarella
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Salt, pepper, oregano

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Chop the onion and garlic and fry in a pan with some oil.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add the tomato sauce and season with salt, pepper and oregano. Let it simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer in a baking dish: lasagna sheets, meat sauce, béchamel sauce. Repeat until all ingredients are used.
  6. Finish with a layer of béchamel and sprinkle with mozzarella.
  7. Bake in the oven for 35-40 minutes until golden brown.
