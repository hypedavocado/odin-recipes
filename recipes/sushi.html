Sushi

Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish made with vinegared rice, fresh fish, and vegetables. Whether you're a beginner or a sushi lover, this recipe will guide you through making delicious sushi rolls at home.

Ingredients (serves 2)

  • 300g sushi rice
  • 350ml water
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 200g salmon or tuna
  • 4 nori sheets
  • ½ cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • Soy sauce, wasabi

Instructions

  1. Rinse the sushi rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Cook the rice with 350ml water. Let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix the rice vinegar through the rice and let it cool completely.
  4. Cut the salmon, cucumber and avocado into thin strips.
  5. Place a nori sheet on a bamboo mat, spread a thin layer of rice on top.
  6. Place the fillings on the edge of the rice.
  7. Roll tightly using the bamboo mat and press firmly.
  8. Cut into slices with a sharp wet knife.
  9. Serve with soy sauce and wasabi.
